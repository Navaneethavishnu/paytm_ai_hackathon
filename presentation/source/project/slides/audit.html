OPS AND AUDIT

Approve in one tab. Every case is kept and replayable.

Ops approvals: evidence pack, masked customer details, and a hash that binds the decision

History: every case with its outcome and rule; filter by merchant or outcome, click to replay

Stored in SQLite: an audit event for every step, with phone numbers and UPI IDs masked · history needs the Ops token

After Approve or Reject the app jumps back to the graph so you see the case finish. History answers “what did the AI do last Tuesday, and who approved it?”: every case is in the audit log and can be replayed step by step. It is Ops-only because it spans all merchants.
